# Individual badge colorways

Review-only revision of the illustrated collection. Six approved SVGs are preserved byte-for-byte. Eighteen newer designs use individual enamel colors and revised symbols, with no interior glow overlays.

- comparison.html: all 24 badges on one responsive sheet, approved versus revised marked.
- index.html: interactive badge preview, changing backgrounds, light/dark and reveal replay.
- palettes.cjs: individual interior/enamel colors for the 18 revisions.
- additional-scenes.cjs: editable filled vector illustrations.
- build.cjs: existing six designs, metal materials, generator and comparison sheet.
- manifest.json: asset IDs, filenames, background colors and availability.

Rebuild with `node assets/achievements/illustrated-v4/build.cjs` from the project root. Listener is reserved until recordings arrive. No changes to native app, unlock rules, or the separate 90 streak checkpoints.
