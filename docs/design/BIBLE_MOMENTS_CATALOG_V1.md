# Closer Bible Moments — consolidated catalog v1

Status: first implementation available in the app. All 78 cards have verified trigger verses, category glows, persistent local unlocks, and a collection in Streaks & Moments. Existing book covers are temporary artwork. Category completion earns a badge; no points or monetary rewards. User decision: one collectible per event, with multiple category tags. Audio work is parked.

## Draft unified copy for merged cards

These paragraphs replace competing versions for the five merged events. They are proposed copy, not a claim of completed theological or reading-level review. Other entries retain the user's supplied copy as their editorial starting point.

**God's Promise to David — A king forever**
God promises David that his family will have a kingdom that lasts forever. Christians see this promise fulfilled in Jesus, who comes from David's family. It gives God's people hope in a King whose rule will never end.

**Suffering for Others — He carries our pain**
Isaiah tells of a servant who suffers for the wrong things others have done. Christians see Jesus in this passage. His love shows us that God does not turn away from our pain. He comes to save us.

**Saul Meets Jesus — An enemy becomes a follower**
Saul is on his way to arrest people who follow Jesus. A bright light stops him, and Jesus speaks to him. Saul's life changes. The man who hurt the church becomes a man who shares the good news about Jesus.

**The Good Shepherd — Known and loved**
Jesus calls Himself the good shepherd. He knows His people and gives His life for them. We are not just faces in a crowd to Him. He knows us, loves us, and calls us to follow Him.

**God Provides — Someone else takes the place**
Abraham sees a ram and offers it instead of his son. He names the place “The Lord Will Provide.” God has made a way when Abraham could not. Christians also see a picture here of Jesus giving His life for others.

## Collection and reward rules

- Persist an unlock by canonical moment ID, not by category, title, translation, or verse.
- Multiple verse entry points resolve to that same ID. Reopening, switching translations, or opening from another category never grants another card or per-card reward.
- Each unlock contributes once to the unique collection total and once to each category it belongs to.
- Category completion uses the tagged totals above: 26, 14, 14, 15, and 14. The overall total remains 78.
- A category reward, if adopted, is keyed separately by category and awarded once. One new card may legitimately finish two categories; that does not create duplicate per-card rewards.
- Reward type/value remains undecided. Proposed approach: small discovery celebration and category-completion badges, without introducing a points economy yet.
- Preserve existing `creation` and `resurrection` unlock IDs. Do not reuse the app's daily-devotional `Moment` state for collectible Bible Moments.

## Build handoff and outstanding content work

1. Pin exact, short glowing verse ranges to the app's real book/verse identifiers. Some submitted references span whole chapters or long parables; do not glow all of them by default.
2. Resolve any shared-verse trigger collisions explicitly. A lookup must not silently return only the first matching collectible.
3. Editorially review every paragraph for fifth-grade readability, scriptural accuracy, and the difference between the passage's words and Christian interpretation. Check chronology claims and inferred details; titles above avoid hard-coding unverified dates.
4. Assign a dedicated artwork asset per card. Existing book covers may serve as explicitly temporary previews. No moment artwork is sourced or generated by this document.
5. Confirm reward details before building reward UI. The no-duplicate-unlock behavior is settled independently of reward type.
6. Implement tapping glowing text to open the top card; retain access to verse highlighting/notes and an accessible named action. The existing long-press moment behavior will need updating.
7. Keep the top card's agreed book/title/importance hierarchy while adding collectible art and verse information deliberately; the expanded card layout is not implemented by this catalog.
8. Preserve reduced-motion behavior, readable text contrast, and category labels in addition to glow colors.
