GENESIS (10)
================================================
In the beginning
Event: Creation
Reference: Genesis 1:1
Category: Turning Points
Tags: Turning Points
Verse text: In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth.
Why it matters: God makes the sky, the land, animals, and people. He calls His creation good. Our world is a gift, and we have a part in caring for it.
ID: creation

A broken world
Event: The Fall
Reference: Genesis 3:6
Category: Turning Points
Tags: Turning Points
Verse text: When the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was a delight to the eyes, and that the tree was to be desired to make one wise, she took some of its fruit, and ate; and she gave some to her husband with her, and he ate it, too.
Why it matters: Adam and Eve turn away from God's command. Their choice brings shame and pain. The story that follows shows God reaching out to a broken world.

Tags: Turning Points

Reference: Genesis 9:13
Category: Turning Points
Tags: Turning Points

Why it matters: After the flood, God gives the rainbow as a sign of His promise. He will never again destroy all life with a flood. The rainbow points to His care for the earth.
